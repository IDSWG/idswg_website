IDSWG HISTORICAL RECORD — REQUEST FOR PAST LEADERS
====================================================

We are building a written history of the IDSWG and would value your account of
the period when you led or were closely involved with the group. Rather than a
list of publications, we are looking for a short narrative — the story behind
the work, in your words.

Please write a short piece (roughly 400 to 700 words) covering whichever of the
following you find most worth recording. You do not need to address every point;
use them as prompts.

  1. THE PERIOD YOU ARE DESCRIBING
     Roughly what years, and what was your role during that time?

  2. WHAT THE GROUP WAS FOCUSED ON
     What were the main priorities, questions, or problems the group was
     working on then? What was the state of innovative trial design at the
     time, and where did the group fit?

  3. A DEFINING EFFORT OR TURNING POINT
     Is there a particular initiative, publication, collaboration, or decision
     from your time that you would point to as especially important? What made
     it matter, and what did it take to get done?

  4. THE PEOPLE
     Who were the key contributors during this period? This helps us credit the
     people who shaped the group, not just the outputs.

  5. WHAT CARRIED FORWARD
     Looking back, what from your era still shapes the group today, or what
     would you want current and future members to understand about how the
     group came to be what it is?

WHAT TO SEND BACK
-----------------
Along with your written piece, please include:

  - Your name and affiliation, as you would like it shown
  - The approximate time period your piece covers (e.g. "2015 to 2019")
  - One sentence summarizing the piece, for the archive listing
  - Optionally, a short list of the key publications or milestones from your
    period that you would like linked or noted alongside the narrative

FORMAT
------
Plain prose is perfect. Write in your own voice — this is a personal account,
not a formal report. Short paragraphs are easier to read than long ones. Do not
worry about formatting, links, or images; we will handle all of that.

We will edit lightly for consistency, format it for the website, and send it
back to you to review before it goes live. Each contribution becomes a featured
entry on the IDSWG History & Achievements page, building a chronological record
of the group told by the people who led it.

Thank you for helping preserve this history.
