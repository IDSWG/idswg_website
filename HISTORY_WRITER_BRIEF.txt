IDSWG HISTORY SERIES — WHAT TO SEND ME
========================================

Thanks for contributing to the IDSWG history series. Each quarter we feature
one short reflection from a member. Here is everything you need to write yours.
Just send me the finished text (email or Word document is fine) and I will
put it on the website.


WHAT TO WRITE
-------------
A short reflection, 300 to 600 words, on the group's history: how something
came to be, a challenge the group faced, the people involved, or how the work
has changed over time. Pick one moment or theme rather than trying to cover
everything. Write in your own voice, this is a personal reflection, not a
formal report. Assume the reader knows the field but not this specific group.


WHAT TO SEND ME
---------------
Please include these four things at the top of your submission:

  1. TITLE
     The headline of your piece.
     Example: "The Early Years of the Randomization Working Group"

  2. YOUR NAME AND AFFILIATION
     As you would like it to appear.
     Example: "Jane Smith, Ph.D., Georgetown University"

  3. DATE
     The date you are writing it (I will use this to order the archive).
     Example: "March 2026"

  4. ONE-SENTENCE SUMMARY
     A single sentence describing the piece. This appears in the list of
     reflections so readers know what it is about.
     Example: "A look back at how the Randomization Working Group grew from
     an informal discussion into a PSI award-winning initiative."

Then the body of your reflection below those four items.


WRITING TIPS
------------
- Keep paragraphs short, 3 to 5 sentences each.
- Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
- If you want a heading inside your piece, just write it on its own line.
- Don't worry about formatting, links, or images. I will handle all of that.


THAT'S IT
---------
Send it whenever it is ready. I will format it, add it to the website, and let
you know when it is live. It will appear at the top of the History &
Achievements page, with past reflections listed below it.
